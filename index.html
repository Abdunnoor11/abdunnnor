About Me

I am a graduate researcher and software engineer based in Dhaka, Bangladesh, with a strong focus on applying deep learning techniques to medical imaging challenges. My research spans retinal vessel segmentation, low-dose CT reconstruction, and skin lesion analysis.

Currently pursuing my M.S. in Software Engineering (Data Science) at Daffodil International University (CGPA: 3.90/4.0), I have authored and co-authored multiple papers accepted at prestigious venues including PRCV, IJCNN, and ICPR.

Beyond research, I serve as a peer reviewer for PRCV and IJCNN, and actively mentor undergraduate students in their research journeys.
