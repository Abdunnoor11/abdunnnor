About Me

I am a M.Sc. student in Software Engineering (Data Science) at Daffodil International University, with a strong research focus on medical image analysis, computer vision, and generative deep learning. My work spans retinal vessel segmentation, low-dose CT reconstruction using diffusion models, lane detection, and pedestrian intention prediction. I have published in venues including PRCV, IJCNN, and CMC, with additional papers under review at Frontiers in Medicine and PeerJ Computer Science. I also serve as a peer reviewer for PRCV and IJCNN.
